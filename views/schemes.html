Government Schemes & Welfare Programs

Explore various government schemes and welfare programs available for citizens

Available Schemes for Residents

PM Awas Yojana

Provides housing to economically weaker sections and low-income groups with financial assistance.

Eligibility: Income below ₹3 lakh/year

Rythu Bharosa Scheme

Provides ₹13,500 financial aid per year to eligible farmers.

Eligibility: Small and marginal farmers

Pension Kanuka

Monthly pension to senior citizens, widows, and disabled persons.

Eligibility: Age above 60 / Disability / Widow

Arogya Sri Health Card

Covers major health expenses for BPL families at government hospitals.

Eligibility: BPL families with health card
